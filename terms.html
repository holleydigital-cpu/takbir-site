The service

Takbir helps you pray on time: the app calculates prayer times for your city, pauses the apps you choose at the time of the adhan, and offers tasbih, istighfar, verses and a Fajr wake-up alarm.

Prayer times

Prayer times are calculated with recognized astronomical methods. They may differ by a few minutes from your mosque’s. When in doubt, follow your local mosque. We can’t be held responsible for any time difference.

Pausing apps

Pausing uses Apple’s Screen Time. It depends on the permissions you grant and on iOS working properly. You can reopen your apps at any time (tasbih, verse or emergency unlock) or remove the permission in your iPhone Settings.

Religious content

Verse translations come from the Noble Quran Encyclopedia (QuranEnc.com): French translation by Rachid Maach and English translation Saheeh International. Supplications and hadiths are quoted with their source. Takbir is not a religious authority: for any religious question, please ask a person of knowledge.
